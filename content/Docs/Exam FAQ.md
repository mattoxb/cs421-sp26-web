## Retakes

### How does the retake work?

For each of the three midterms, you can elect to retake it the week before finals.  The exam retake allows you to
have a second chance at any question from the corresponding midterm.  If your score improves, we take the new score.
If it does not, we take the original.

### If I'm happy with my midterm score, can I skip the retake?

Yes.

### If I redo a question and do worse, does that affect my grade?

No.  Not this semester anyway; future semesters will have a different formula.

### Will I get the same exam?

All the questions on the exam are randomly selected from a pool of questions.  You might get lucky and get a repeat,
but most likely you are going to see a related but different question.

### How much time will I get to take the retake?

Each one is for 50 minutes, like a standard midterm.

### Can I redo *all* of the exams?

Yes.  You will sign up for all three retakes.

### Can I do all my exams in one session?

Sadly, no.
